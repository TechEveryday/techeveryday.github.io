Privacy Policy

My Assistant Coach

Effective 15 July 2026 · Last updated 7 October 2026

My Assistant Coach collects nothing. It has no accounts, makes no network requests, and contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising. Nothing you type into it ever reaches us, because there is nowhere for it to go.

What we collect

Nothing. Not your name, not your email, not your location, not an advertising identifier, not crash reports, not usage statistics. We do not have a server, and the app does not talk to one. Our App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, and it is accurate.

What you type into the app, including players' names

To build a roster you will type in real information about real people — usually children: their names, their shirt numbers, who plays in goal, where they like to play. As you run matches the app also keeps a record of them — who was on the field, in which positions, and for how many minutes. We want to be specific about this rather than hide behind "we collect no data".

That information is stored only on your device, by the app, for the app. It is never transmitted anywhere. We never receive it, cannot see it, and could not produce it if we were asked to. It is not backed up to any service of ours, not synced between devices, and not shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and everything it stored on your device goes with it. There is no account to close and no request to send us, because we are not holding a copy. You can also remove individual teams and players inside the app at any time.

Your device's own backups

One thing is outside the app, and we would rather say so than let "stored only on your device" imply more than it does. If you back up your iPhone or iPad — to iCloud, or to a computer — Apple includes the app's data in that backup, the same as it does for most apps. That copy belongs to your Apple account and is governed by Apple's privacy policy; it never passes through us, and we cannot access it.

It does mean that deleting the app does not erase a backup you made earlier, and restoring that backup can bring your rosters back. To remove them entirely, delete the teams inside the app first, or manage your backups in your device's settings.

Cookies and similar technologies

None. The app sets no cookies, uses no advertising identifiers, and contains no tracking pixels or fingerprinting. The only storage it uses is the device's own app storage, to keep your rosters between launches — that is functional storage the app cannot work without, it stays on the device, and nothing reads it but the app.

These web pages set no cookies either, load no scripts, and make no requests to any other site. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

Children's privacy

This app is built for coaches of youth teams, and rosters will routinely contain children's names. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — or from anyone — because we do not collect personal information at all. No information about any child reaches us or any third party through this app.

If you are a coach, note that the roster on your device is your responsibility in the same way a paper team sheet in your bag would be. The app's protection is that the information never leaves the device; keeping the device itself secure is up to you.

Age

The app is meant for coaches, parents and other adults who run youth teams, not for children to use themselves. It has no accounts and no sign-up, so there is no age to verify and no parental consent to collect: under laws such as the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, consent is required before an operator collects a child's personal information, and we collect none. Players' names stay on the coach's device.

Third parties

There are none. No analytics provider, no advertising network, no crash reporter, no cloud backend, no software development kit that reports to anyone. We do not sell or share information, because we do not have any.

Apple may collect its own information when you download the app from the App Store — that is between you and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy, and we receive nothing from it beyond aggregate download counts that identify no one.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and revise the date above before that version ships. Given that "collects nothing" is the point of the app, we do not expect this to change.
