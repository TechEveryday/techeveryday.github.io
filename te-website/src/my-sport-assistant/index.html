My Assistant Coach

A sideline app for youth coaches. Build a roster, place a lineup, run the match clock, plan substitutions, sketch plays. Soccer, basketball and baseball.

Works with no signal, because it never needed one. No accounts, no network requests, no analytics. Nothing about your team leaves your phone.

Common questions

Where is my data? Can I get it back after deleting the app?
On your device only — nothing is backed up to us, because we have no server. Deleting the app deletes its data from the device. The one way back is your own device backup: if you backed up to iCloud or a computer before deleting, restoring that backup restores the app's data too.

Can I use it on the sideline with no reception?
Yes. That is what it is for. The app never makes a network request, so a dead signal changes nothing about how it behaves.

Does the clock keep time if I lock my phone?
Yes. Time is read from the device clock rather than counted while the app is open, so locking the phone or switching apps mid-match does not lose or slow the match time, and neither do the per-player minutes.

Nothing moved when I made a substitution.
That is deliberate. Substitutions are planned first: you set up the changes, read the plain-English summary of what will happen, and nothing on the field moves until you confirm. Cancelling discards the plan.

Can I run more than one team?
Yes — as many as you like. Each keeps its own sport, format and shape, and you switch between them in the app.
